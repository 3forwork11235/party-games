## Short description (<= 100)

## Full description (<= 1000)
"Who of Us?" is a fun party game for 3 to 10 friends. A question appears on screen: "Who would be the first to escape a desert island?" Everyone secretly votes for one of the players, then tries to guess who the majority will pick.

How to play:
1. One player creates a room and shares the code. Everyone else joins by code from their phone or computer.
2. The room host picks the question packs and starts the game.
3. Each of the 10 rounds lasts 20 seconds: choose who you vote for and guess who will get the most votes.
4. Then comes the reveal: vote bars grow one by one, and players who guessed right score points.
5. At the end, enjoy the podium and fun titles: see who your friends picked most often.

Question packs: "Friends" and "Family" are free from the start, while "Work", "Absurd" and "Party" can be unlocked. All questions are harmless and suitable for any age. Play in English, Russian or Kazakh.
