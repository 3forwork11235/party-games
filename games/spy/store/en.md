## Full description (<= 1000)
"Spy" is a party game for 3 to 8 friends. Everyone knows the secret location, such as "Hospital", and gets a role there. Everyone except the spy, who has no idea where they are and has to pretend.

How to play:
1. One player creates a room and shares the code. Everyone else joins by code from their phone or computer.
2. The room host picks the location packs and the number of rounds, then starts the game.
3. Everyone sees their role card. The spy sees a board of 24 locations instead.
4. Players take turns asking each other questions for 5 minutes: expose the spy without giving the location away.
5. When everyone is ready, voting begins. If the spy gets caught, they get one last chance.
6. The spy can name the location early: a right guess wins the round, a wrong one loses it.

120 locations in 6 themed packs: three are free from the start, the rest can be unlocked. All locations are harmless and suitable for any age. Play in English, Russian or Kazakh.
