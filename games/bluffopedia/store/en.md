## Название (≤ 50)
Bluffopedia

## Короткое описание (≤ 100)
Invent a believable lie and spot the truth among your friends' fakes. Online for 3–8 players.

## Полное описание (≤ 1000)
Bluffopedia is a party game for 3 to 8 players. A rare word or a strange fact appears with a blank: "The Japanese word "tsundoku" means the habit of buying books and ___". Everyone secretly writes a believable fake answer, then all the options are shuffled together with the real one. Your goal: find the truth and don't fall for your friends' lies.

How to play:
1. One player creates a room and shares the code. Others join by code from their phones or computers.
2. The host picks packs, the number of questions (5, 7 or 10) and the question language, then starts the game.
3. Each round you get 45 seconds to write your fake and 30 seconds to vote for the option you think is true.
4. Then the options are revealed one by one: who picked what and who wrote each lie. You score for finding the truth and for every friend your fake fooled.
5. At the end there is a podium: find out who the most convincing liar is.

Play in Russian, Kazakh or English.
